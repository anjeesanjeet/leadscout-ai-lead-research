## Research pipeline

flowchart TD

    extract_profile --> verify_facts
    score_fit["Score fit against the segment rubric<br/><small>score computed in code; 'yes' needs evidence</small>"]
    verify_facts --> score_fit
    draft_email["Draft outreach from verified facts<br/><small>length, banned phrases, placeholders checked; 1 retry</small>"]
    FIT{"Fit score ≥ 6?"}
    score_fit --> FIT
